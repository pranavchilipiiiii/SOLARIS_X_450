A flight system that shows its work.

THE TEAM

Who built this.

Solaris X-450 is a two-person build: one founder driving software, development, and direction, and one focused on the physical design and construction of the drone itself.

FOUNDER & DEVELOPER

DESIGNER & BUILDER

Pranav Reddy Chilipi

SOPHOMORE, LEGACY RANCH HIGH SCHOOL

Founder and Developer of Solaris X450. My interest in engineering started after my first high-school engineering class, where I discovered how much I enjoyed designing, building, and experimenting with new ideas — an interest robotics helped deepen. Looking for a meaningful engineering project, I landed on the idea of a solar-powered drone as a way to explore combining renewable energy with drone technology. Solaris X450 is my first major high-school engineering project, which makes it especially meaningful to me. I focus on the software, development, technology, and overall direction of the project. I'm also certified in Autodesk and Fusion 360. Through this build I want to keep developing my engineering skills, solve real-world challenges, and turn an idea into a working prototype.

BUILDER & DESIGNER

Shriyan Vemuri

SOPHOMORE, LEGACY RANCH HIGH SCHOOL

Builder and Designer of Solaris X450. My interest started when I first did robotics in sixth grade, and I liked it right away. That interest grew from there, including competing with a robotics team at the 2025 state competition — experience that taught me a lot about teamwork, design, and engineering. I joined Solaris X450 to take that interest further by helping build a project that combines drone technology, renewable energy, design, and construction. I'm also certified in Autodesk and Fusion 360, and I focus on the physical design, construction, and development of the drone. Through this build I want to strengthen my skills in robotics, mechatronics, and engineering design while helping turn our concept into a working prototype.
